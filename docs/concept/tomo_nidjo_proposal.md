# Tomo & Niđo: concept step 1 (proposal)

Concept sheets (flat doodle, thick black outlines, no shading, ~8 heads; drawn procedurally by `draw_concepts.py`):
`docs/concept/tomo_v1.png`, `docs/concept/nidjo_v1.png`, `docs/concept/nidjo_v2.png` (v2: wide open jacket showing the red tartan lining; the 3D model follows v2) (slug `nidjo`, display name **Niđo**).

## Tomo: lean damage hero (tall, narrow silhouette)
Look: black beanie, short dark beard, black tee, dark shorts, accent **red** (sneakers + laptop sticker).
Alt outfit (later skin): striped sweater, plaid trousers, red sneakers.
- **Primary: Packet Burst.** Fast three-round bursts of glowing data packets; accurate at mid range.
- **Ability 1: Hotfix.** Short blink forward that leaves an afterimage; the next burst deals bonus damage.
- **Ability 2: Breakpoint.** Throws a marker that freezes (roots) the first enemy it hits for a moment.
- **Ultimate: Force Push.** Channels a wide shockwave of code that knocks enemies back and damages them.

## Niđo: sturdy bruiser / off-tank (broad, boxy silhouette)
Look: mostly bald (thin buzz, high receding hairline; v2), light goatee, green Harrington jacket with red tartan lining, navy/white striped tee,
black trousers, black belt bag. Accent **green** (the red lining is a small secondary colour because the
outfit requires it). No shirt prints.
- **Primary: Ping Hammer.** Short-range swings with a heavy network-switch hammer; every third hit pulses a small knockback.
- **Ability 1: Firewall.** Raises a frontal energy shield that blocks shots for a few seconds.
- **Ability 2: Stand-up Meeting.** Pulls nearby enemies a short distance toward him and slows them.
- **Ultimate: Rollback.** Restores nearby allies' health to what it was a few seconds ago and gives Niđo brief damage reduction.
